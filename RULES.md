# Cube Chess Rules

## Standard Chess

Standard is traditional one-versus-one chess on a single 8x8 floor plane. Pieces cannot climb or move onto another face. Traditional castling, en passant, promotion, checkmate, stalemate, repetition, fifty-move, and insufficient-material rules apply.

## Cube formats

1. Each army begins with a traditional chess formation on its home face.
2. Every piece begins plane-bound and cannot cross an edge.
3. A piece becomes gateway-ready by ending a move on the opponent's back rank.
4. On a later turn, a gateway-ready piece may climb through that forward edge.
5. After climbing, the piece becomes cube-enabled and may continue legal movement across connected faces and edges.
6. A pawn reaching the opponent's back rank promotes to a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight. The promoted piece remains gateway-ready.
7. CPU-controlled pawns currently promote to a Queen by default.

## Draws and resignation

- A draw offer succeeds only when every active opposing player or team accepts.
- Resigned and eliminated armies do not vote on draw offers.
- In Standard, resignation awards the match to the opponent.
- In Coalition and Teams, a resigned army is removed; play continues while multiple teams remain.
- In Free-for-All, a resigned army becomes inactive and all of its future turns are skipped. Its pieces remain on their current squares.

## Victory

Kings are checkmated rather than captured. In multiplayer formats, eliminated armies are skipped. The match ends when only one active team or Free-for-All player remains.
